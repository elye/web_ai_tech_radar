# Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image)

## Overview
Nano Banana Pro, officially known as **Gemini 3 Pro Image**, is Google DeepMind's latest advanced image generation and editing model. Released in November 2025, it builds upon the Gemini 3 architecture to deliver state-of-the-art visual capabilities with deep reasoning.

## Key Benefits
- **High Resolution:** Native support for 1K, 2K, and 4K visuals (up to 5632 x 3072 pixels).
- **Text Rendering:** Superior ability to generate accurate, stylized text in multiple languages.
- **Image Fusion:** Blends up to 14 reference images and maintains character consistency for up to 5 subjects.
- **Advanced Editing:** Professional-grade control over camera angles, lighting, and depth of field.
- **Thinking Mode:** Uses a "thinking" process to refine compositions via interim thought images.
- **Google Search Integration:** Verifies facts and generates imagery based on real-time data.

## When to Use
- Creating high-fidelity marketing assets and infographics with legible text.
- Character design requiring consistency across multiple scenes.
- Data-driven visualizations verified by Google Search.
- Complex creative workflows needing granular control over scene composition.

## Cost
**Freemium** - Available in Gemini Advanced and for developers via API.

## Status
**Ring: TRIAL** - A powerful new entrant in image generation, offering unique reasoning and consistency features.
